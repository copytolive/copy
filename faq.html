🚀 Getting Started
What is CopyToLive?

CopyToLive is a copy-trading platform connected to Hyperliquid. You subscribe to vetted algorithmic strategies; our system executes their signals on YOUR Hyperliquid account using YOUR API key (with trade-only permission).

How is this different from a trading bot?

Traditional bots use one strategy. CopyToLive curates 100+ strategies that pass our Tier-4 backtest filter (PF 2-8, >300 trades, drawdown <25%). You can follow multiple strategies with custom risk limits per strategy.

Do I need crypto/Hyperliquid experience?

Basic familiarity helps. We provide an 8-video onboarding (connect wallet → pick strategy → adjust risk → emergency pause). If you're new to perps, start with paper trading for 2+ weeks.

🔐 Security & Funds
Do you hold my crypto?

NO. Your funds stay on your Hyperliquid account. We only have a trade-permission API key (encrypted with AES-256-GCM) — we cannot withdraw. You can revoke our key anytime via Hyperliquid settings.

How is my API key protected?
  • Encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) with per-user data key
  • Master key stored in HSM-equivalent isolated env, never in code/repo
  • Never shown again after entering — only masked display
  • Audit log every decryption with IP + timestamp
Can I use 2FA?

Yes — TOTP (Google Authenticator / Authy) is available in /account/security. Strongly recommended.

What if your platform is hacked?

Even if attackers got our database, they can't steal your funds because we don't have withdrawal permission. They could only place trades — your daily loss limit (default 10%) caps damage. We have a kill-switch that pauses ALL copy-trading globally if breach is detected.

📊 Trading & Strategies
How do I know strategies are real?

Our /track-record.html page shows verifiable backtest data (with audit hash) for top strategies. After live attach, we publish live performance separately so you can compare backtest vs reality.

What's the expected return?

Historically, top-tier strategies range from +3% to +15% monthly, with drawdowns of 10-25%. These are NOT guarantees — past performance does not predict future results. See /methodology.html for our calculator.

Can I lose money?

YES. Crypto perps are high-risk. You can lose more than your initial deposit due to leverage. Read our full Risk Disclosure before live trading. Set a daily loss limit and never risk capital you can't afford to lose entirely.

How do I limit risk?
  • Max leverage cap (default 5x, max 20x)
  • Position size as % of wallet (default 20%)
  • Daily loss limit % (default 10% → auto-pause)
  • Per-trade max USD
  • Emergency kill-switch in /account/safety
